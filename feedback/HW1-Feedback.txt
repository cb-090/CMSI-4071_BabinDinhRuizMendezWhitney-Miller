      discussion of the ValuJet Flight 592 crash.  The summary captures

      recounting details than analyzing the article's overarching message
      about complexity and organizational responsibility.

   The essay makes several legitimate connections between the article
      and software engineering, such as:
      * Recognition that complex systems require fault tolerance.
      * Discussion of scaling systems and anticipating failure cases.
      * Consideration of engineering solutions that reduce human error.
      * Reflection on business pressures versus customer safety.
   These are appropriate software-engineering themes.  However, the

      half reads as an application of concepts rather than providing
      explicit personal reflection.
